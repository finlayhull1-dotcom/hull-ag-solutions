Import precision map

GeoJSON: Point, Polygon or MultiPolygon features with numeric properties. CSV: latitude/longitude (or lat/lon), plus a numeric value column. Coordinates must be WGS84 decimal degrees. Select the measurement column after import.

Imports are saved in this browser. Large high-resolution files may exceed browser storage; export your farm backup regularly. Machinery proprietary formats and raster TIFFs require conversion to GeoJSON or CSV first.

Paddock gross margins

Gross margin = crop income minus recorded variable inputs and job costs. Job costs are estimates entered by you, not verified invoices. Excludes overheads, tax and unentered costs.

Data & settings

Back up your farm data

All information is saved in this browser using local storage. It is not synchronised between phone and computer, and clearing browser data may delete it.

No accounts, cloud storage, notifications or live agronomist collaboration yet. This is a working local prototype, not a production farm recordkeeping system.

Cloud sync — optional beta

Connect a Supabase project to securely store a copy of your farm data under your own account. No data leaves this browser until you sign in and click an upload button.

Upload replaces your existing cloud snapshot; download replaces this browser's data. Export a JSON backup before either operation. This beta is a single-account snapshot, not shared multi-user editing or automatic conflict resolution.
